# Watering

Water in the morning, at the base of the plant, not on the leaves. #technique

## Schedule

| Plant            | How often           |
| ---------------- | ------------------- |
| [[tomatoes]]     | every two days      |
| [[courgettes]]   | once a week, deeply |
| [[strawberries]] | every three days    |

## Tips

A layer of [[mulching|mulch]] keeps the soil moist for longer.
