# Tomatoes

Sow indoors in March, plant out after the last frost.

Pinch out the side shoots every week, so the plant puts its energy into fruit. ^side-shoots

Grow them next to [[basil]]: it keeps whitefly away, and ends up in the [[pesto]] anyway. See [[companion-planting]].

## Problems

- [[aphids]] on the young shoots
- Curling leaves: check the [[watering]]
